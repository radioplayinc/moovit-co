Moovit — Moving questions

Moving questions, answered.

Plain answers before you book. If you are ready, the booking itself takes a few minutes: an instant estimate, then a booking reference.

How booking works

  1. Choose whether you need stuff moved.
  2. Answer a few questions about move size, ZIP codes, date, stairs, packing, and heavy items.
  3. Review your instant itemized estimate.
  4. Leave your name, email, and phone to get your booking reference.

No card is collected on the site and no payment is due today. Your invoice is sent by email or text message.

What it costs

There is no single flat price, because no two moves are the same. The estimate is built from the size of your move, the distance between ZIP codes, stairs, packing help, and any especially heavy items. You see the itemized estimate before you commit, and the final price is confirmed after a quick route review.

Service area

Enter your pickup and drop-off ZIP codes at the start of the booking. The estimate shows the driving distance for your route, and the booking is never blocked by distance.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a move cost?

It depends on the size of your move, the distance between ZIP codes, stairs, packing help, and any especially heavy items. Start a booking to see an instant itemized estimate. The final price is confirmed after a quick route review.

How do I book a move?

Choose whether you need stuff moved, answer a few questions about size, ZIP codes, and date, review your instant estimate, then leave your name, email, and phone. You will get a booking reference right away.

Do I have to pay by card on the site?

No. No card is collected on the site and no payment is due today. Your invoice is sent by email or text message, and you pay whenever you are ready.

What areas do you serve?

Enter your pickup and drop-off ZIP codes at the start of the booking. The estimate shows the driving distance for your route, and the booking is never blocked by distance.

Do you handle stairs, packing, and heavy items?

Yes. The booking asks about stairs on the route, whether you need help packing, and anything especially heavy, so the estimate and the crew plan reflect the real job.

How far ahead should I book?

Pick your preferred date and time window during the booking. If your date is close, book as soon as you can so a crew can be confirmed for it.

Can I get a quote without booking?

Yes. You can choose the quote-only option at the lock-in step and still get your estimate and reference without reserving a crew.

For movers
